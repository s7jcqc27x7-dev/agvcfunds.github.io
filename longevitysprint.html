Helping longevity innovations reach new markets.

助力长寿创新走向新市场。

We work alongside founders, researchers, and industry partners to help promising innovations move from scientific development toward commercial adoption. Our work combines cross-border acceleration, manufacturing and licensing partnerships, and hands-on venture building.

我们与创始人、科研人员及产业伙伴并肩合作，推动具潜力的创新从科研开发走向商业应用。我们的工作涵盖跨境加速、生产与授权合作，以及深度参与的企业共建。

01

Cross-Border Acceleration

跨境加速

We help science-led teams establish a presence in new markets, connect with relevant partners, and develop practical commercialization strategies.

我们协助以科学为核心的团队在新市场建立业务据点、对接相关合作伙伴，并制定切实可行的商业化策略。

  • Market entry planning and company setup
  • Corporate structuring and strategy, with accounting and tax handled through professional advisers
  • Research, hospital and clinical partnerships, including CROs
  • IP commercialization coordination and R&D collaboration
  • Funding and grant navigation
  • Introductions to buyers, manufacturers and distributors
  • 市场进入规划与公司设立
  • 企业架构与战略规划，会计及税务事宜由专业顾问协同处理
  • 对接研究机构、医院及临床合作伙伴（包括CRO）
  • 统筹知识产权商业化与研发合作
  • 资金与政府资助渠道指引
  • 引荐买家、制造商及分销商
Best for
适合对象
Science-led teams entering a new market
进入新市场、以科学为核心的团队
02

CDMO & Manufacturing Partnerships

CDMO与生产合作

We connect biotech, pharmaceutical, and MedTech companies with suitable manufacturing, development, and licensing partners across Asia.

我们为生物科技、制药及医疗器械企业，对接亚洲合适的生产、研发与授权合作伙伴。

  • Identifying and screening suitable CDMO and manufacturing partners
  • CDMO introductions and supply-agreement support
  • Licensing coordination with regional pharmaceutical, MedTech and manufacturing partners
  • Deal-structuring support, including milestone frameworks
  • Manufacturing readiness, including quality-system preparation
  • Regulatory submissions coordinated with qualified advisers
  • R&D collaboration with local academic and research institutes
  • 寻找并筛选合适的CDMO及生产合作伙伴
  • 引荐CDMO并支持供应协议洽谈
  • 统筹与区域制药、医疗器械及制造伙伴的授权合作
  • 协助交易架构设计，包括里程碑安排
  • 生产准备工作，包括质量体系筹备
  • 与合资格顾问协同推进法规申报
  • 与本地高校及研究机构开展研发合作
Best for
适合对象
Teams preparing to manufacture or license in Asia
准备在亚洲生产或授权的团队
03

Venture Building & Operational Support

企业共建与运营支持

For founders who need more hands-on support, we work alongside their teams on operational planning, strategic partnerships, commercialization, and business development.

对于需要更深度支持的创始人，我们与其团队并肩合作，推进运营规划、战略合作、商业化及业务拓展。

  • Embedded operational leadership through an External CXO model
  • Operational planning across procurement, manufacturing and distribution
  • Strategic partnerships with industry, academic institutions and the public sector
  • Commercialization and business development across health-tech, medical-device and supplement categories
  • Working alongside founding teams from before incorporation
  • 以外部CXO模式提供嵌入式运营领导
  • 覆盖采购、生产与分销的运营规划
  • 与产业界、学术机构及公共部门建立战略合作
  • 健康科技、医疗器械及营养补充品领域的商业化与业务拓展
  • 在公司注册前即与创始团队并肩合作
Best for
适合对象
Founders who want hands-on operating support
需要实操型运营支持的创始人
