Justin Tso
Founder & General Partner
创始人兼普通合伙人

Justin Tso is the Founder and General Partner of Ag Ventures and Director of Longevity Global China. He holds an MA in Biotechnology from Columbia University. His experience spans biotech IPO and SPAC advisory involving the Hong Kong Stock Exchange, as well as cross-border healthcare venture work with MSQ Ventures, RTW Investments, and emerging AI and biotechnology companies. He supports fundraising, licensing, and commercialization and helps connect aging scientists, clinicians, entrepreneurs, and investors through longevity-focused initiatives across Asia.

Justin Tso 是 Ag Ventures 创始人兼普通合伙人，并担任 Longevity Global 中国区总监。他拥有哥伦比亚大学生物技术硕士学位，曾参与涉及香港交易所的生物科技 IPO 及 SPAC 顾问工作，并先后与 MSQ Ventures、RTW Investments 以及多家新兴人工智能与生物科技企业开展跨境医疗健康创投工作。他协助企业推进融资、授权与商业化，并通过一系列聚焦长寿的项目，在亚洲连接衰老科学家、临床医生、创业者与投资人。

Aaisha Bhuiyan
General Partner
普通合伙人

Aaisha Bhuiyan is a General Partner at Ag Ventures, a Venture Partner at Hustle Fund, and co-founder of Emerging Founder School. She is also a limited partner in two venture funds and an active angel investor in early-stage healthcare and B2B SaaS companies. Alongside her investment work, she co-owns Next Day Access of Bergen County, which provides accessibility solutions that support aging in place. Aaisha also teaches as an adjunct faculty member in Columbia University's Strategic Communication program.

Aaisha Bhuiyan 是 Ag Ventures 普通合伙人、Hustle Fund 风险合伙人，以及 Emerging Founder School 联合创始人。她同时是两支风险投资基金的有限合伙人，并积极以天使投资人身份投资早期医疗健康与 B2B SaaS 企业。在投资工作之外，她与合伙人共同经营 Next Day Access of Bergen County，提供支持居家养老的无障碍解决方案。她还在哥伦比亚大学策略传播项目担任兼任教员。
