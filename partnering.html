Partnering

合作伙伴

Building the future of healthy aging, together.

携手推动长寿科技创新。

We work with early-stage companies and research teams developing solutions for longer, healthier lives. Our focus spans four areas of longevity innovation, with particular interest in teams that have a clear scientific foundation, access to relevant intellectual property, and a practical path to commercialization.

我们与科研团队、初创企业及产业机构合作，关注具有科学依据和商业化潜力的技术与产品，协助创新项目从研发走向市场。
